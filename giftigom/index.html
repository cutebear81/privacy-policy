Google이 처리하는 광고 정보의 보유 기간은 Google 개인정보처리방침을 따릅니다. 5. 사용자의 권리와 선택 등록한 쿠폰 정보의 열람, 수정, 삭제 카메라, 사진 라이브러리, 알림, 추적 허용 등 권한의 허용 또는 해제(기기 설정) 맞춤 광고 끄기: 아이폰은 기기 설정 > 개인정보 보호 및 보안 > 추적에서 기프티곰의 추적을 끌 수 있습니다. 안드로이드는 기기 설정 > 개인정보 보호(또는 Google) > 광고에서 광고 ID를 삭제할 수 있습니다. 광고 제거: 앱 설정에서 '광고 제거'를 구매하면 광고가 표시되지 않습니다. 앱 삭제를 통한 기기 데이터 일괄 삭제 6. 만 14세 미만 아동의 개인정보 본 앱은 만 14세 미만 아동을 대상으로 하는 서비스가 아닙니다. 7. 처리방침의 변경 본 처리방침은 법령, 정책 또는 서비스 변경에 따라 수정될 수 있으며, 변경 시 본 페이지를 통해 공지합니다. 변경된 처리방침은 게시된 날부터 효력이 발생합니다. 8. 문의처 개인정보 처리에 관한 문의사항은 아래로 연락주시기 바랍니다. 이메일: help@tonyne.kr Giftigom Privacy Policy (English) Last updated: November 15, 2026 Effective date: November 15, 2026 Giftigom ("the App") lets you manage coupon images and get expiration reminders. Coupon data you add is stored only on your device and, if you turn on iCloud backup, in your own backup space (iCloud on iPhone, the app-only folder of Google Drive on Android). Tonyne does not receive or store it. To keep the App free, it shows Google AdMob banner ads, and Google processes the information needed for ads as described below. 1. Information We Process 1-1. Information you provide Coupon images you take with the camera or choose from your photo library Coupon details you enter (name, expiration date, memo, etc.) This information is stored on your device and, if you use backup, in your own iCloud (iPhone) or the app-only folder of your Google Drive (Android). It is never sent to Tonyne or to advertising companies. 1-2. Device permissions Camera: to photograph coupons. Photo library: to choose existing images as coupons. Notifications: to alert you before a coupon expires. Tracking (App Tracking Transparency, iPhone): used only to show personalized ads. You can use every feature of the App without allowing it. Google sign-in (Android, optional): used only for Google Drive backup and restore, with access limited to the App's own folder. You can turn these off at any time in your device settings. 1-3. Advertising (Google AdMob) To show ads, Google AdMob may collect and process: device identifiers (iPhone: the advertising identifier IDFA, only if you allow tracking; Android: the Google advertising ID), approximate location (based on IP address), ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks, and diagnostic data such as crashes and performance. This is used to serve ads, measure ad performance and prevent fraud, under Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy How Google uses information from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites Users in the EEA, the UK and similar regions see a consent screen before ads are shown. Coupon images and coupon details are never shared with advertising companies. 1-4. Information we do not collect The App does not collect your name, email address, phone number or other personal identifiers, nor contacts, calendars or other data on your device. 2. Storage Coupon data stays on your device. If you enable iCloud backup in the App settings, a backup file is saved in your own iCloud Drive (Giftigom folder) under Apple's iCloud terms. On Android, Google Drive backup saves to the app-only folder of your own Google Drive under Google's terms, and Android's device backup may include coupon data and App settings. Tonyne does not operate any server for the App. 3. Sharing Tonyne does not share or sell your coupon data. Only the information in 1-3 is collected and processed by Google for advertising. 4. Retention and deletion Coupon data is kept until you delete it in the App or delete the App. You can delete iCloud backup files in iCloud Drive, and Google Drive backups under Manage apps in Google Drive settings. Advertising data processed by Google is retained under Google's Privacy Policy. 5. Your choices View, edit and delete your coupons. Allow or revoke camera, photo library, notification and tracking permissions in device settings. Turn off personalized ads: on iPhone, Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; on Android, delete the advertising ID in Settings > Privacy (or Google) > Ads. Remove ads: purchase "Remove Ads" in the App settings. Delete all on-device data by deleting the App. 6. Children The App is not directed to children under 14. 7. Changes We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page and take effect when posted. 8. Contact help@tonyne.kr
